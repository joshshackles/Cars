# CARS Driver Mobile Privacy Notes

This draft is for the Google Play privacy policy and Data safety form. Review with program leadership before publishing.

## Purpose

CARS Driver helps assigned volunteer drivers view trips, navigate to pickup/dropoff locations, report issues, and submit mileage automatically.

## Data Collected

- Account information: driver name and email.
- Trip information: assigned ride details, pickup/dropoff addresses, rider phone number, approved rider notes, and trip status.
- Location data: foreground GPS location while an assigned trip is active.
- Device/session information: mobile session token, device name, platform, and last-used timestamp.
- Support/safety data: decline reasons and issue reports submitted by the driver.

## Location Use

The app requests foreground location permission. Location is collected only when the driver starts GPS tracking for an assigned trip. Location pings are sent to CARS Dispatch to:

- Record trip progress.
- Calculate mileage from start to finish.
- Provide dispatch and finance audit records.

The MVP does not request background location permission.

## Data Sharing

Data is used by the CARS Dispatch organization operating the transportation program. It is not sold. It may be shared internally with authorized dispatch, finance, reporting, and administrative users for transportation operations, reimbursement, safety, and compliance.

## Retention

Trip, mileage, audit, and location records are retained according to the organization&apos;s transportation program record-retention policy.

## User Controls

Drivers can deny location permission, but automatic trip mileage cannot be calculated without GPS. Drivers can sign out to revoke the local mobile session.
